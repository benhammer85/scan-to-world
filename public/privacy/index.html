Warm to the Touch

Privacy

October 2026

Warm to the Touch collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the game never sends anything over the internet.

What stays on your phone

  • Your worlds and how far you've got, so you can come back to them. They are kept only on your device, and deleting the app deletes them.
  • A few settings, such as the ink you chose and whether you tip or breathe.

Motion

When you tip to pour, the game reads how your phone is tilted. It uses this only in the moment, to move the lava, and never stores or sends it.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. It collects no information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will say so first, with a new date at the top.
