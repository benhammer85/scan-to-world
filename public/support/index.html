How to play

  • Hold the phone level, and heat gathers under the smoke.
  • Tip the phone to pour. A faint line of dots shows where the lava will run.
  • Tip when the smoke is heavy, and it bursts, throwing ash.
  • Drag to turn the world. Hold a finger on it to keep the heat in.

If you'd rather not tip, choose Breathe under "more" on the opening card. The lava then pours by itself, a breath at a time, and you turn the world to choose where it goes.

Common questions

The lava doesn't pour when I tip. The game takes however you held the phone when it opened as level. Hold it as you'd like to play, then reopen the game.

Where are my worlds kept? On your phone only. Deleting the app deletes them.

Is there sound? No. The game is meant to be quiet. On iPhone you feel it instead, through gentle haptics.
