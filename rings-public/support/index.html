A ring a day

How it works

Choose a wood and plant its seed. Each day, draw one loop around the tree: how loosely you draw it decides how good a year it was. A year after the first ring, the tree stands in your grove, and a new one begins.

Seeing it printed

Hold your finger still on the slice for a moment to see it printed on a sheet of its own. “keep this print” saves or shares it.

Keeping your trees safe

Your trees live only on your phone. Pinch out to the grove and touch “keep a copy” now and then: it saves everything as a file, wherever you like. “take one back” restores it, on this phone or a new one.

Your weather

In the grove, “bring your weather” lets the grove show the weather where you are. See privacy for what that sends.
