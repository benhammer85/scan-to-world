Well Rounded

Privacy

October 2026

Well Rounded keeps your trees on your phone and nowhere else. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

What's kept, and where

Each ring you draw, the day it was drawn, the wood you chose and the trees in your grove are kept in your phone's own storage for this app. They never leave it unless you choose to keep a copy, which saves them as a file wherever you put it.

Your weather, only if you ask

If you touch “bring your weather” in the grove, the app asks for your location. It rounds it to about ten kilometres and sends only that to Open-Meteo, a free weather service, at most once an hour, to learn the weather there. Your rough location and the last weather are kept on your phone so the grove opens as it was. Touch it again (“your weather”) and both are forgotten, and nothing more is sent. Open-Meteo's own privacy terms are at open-meteo.com/en/terms.

Nothing else

The app's typefaces and pictures are part of the app; nothing else is fetched from anywhere. Saving or sharing a print uses your phone's own share sheet, and goes only where you send it.

Children

The app collects nothing from anyone, children included.

If this ever changes, this page will say so first.
